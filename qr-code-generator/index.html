QR Code Generator

Generate QR codes from text or URLs for easy sharing and scanning.

How to Use
  1. Enter the text, URL, or content you want to encode in the text area
  2. Choose your preferred QR code size and format
  3. Select an error correction level (higher levels allow more damage tolerance)
  4. Click "Generate QR Code" to create your QR code
  5. Download the QR code image or regenerate with different settings
